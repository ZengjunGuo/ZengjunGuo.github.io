About Me个人简介

I am a master’s student in Territorial Spatial Planning at the School of Urban Planning and Design, Peking University, advised by Dr. Junqing Tang. I received my bachelor’s degree in Urban Management from Nankai University and was an exchange student in Urban Studies at the University of Glasgow.我目前在北京大学城市规划与设计学院攻读国土空间规划专业硕士学位，导师为汤俊卿博士。此前，我在南开大学获得城市管理专业管理学学士学位，并曾赴英国格拉斯哥大学城市研究专业交流学习。

My research examines the coupled relationships between human activity, urban space, and disaster risk. I study how compound hazards affect critical infrastructure and how infrastructure disruption interacts with human mobility and protective responses. This question connects my work on global road systems, urban healthcare provision, neighborhood accessibility, and disaster early warning.我的研究围绕人类活动—城市空间—灾害风险的耦合关系展开，关注复合灾害如何影响关键基础设施，以及基础设施受损如何与人群移动、避险响应相互作用。这一问题贯穿我在全球道路系统、城市医疗服务、社区可达性和灾害预警方面的研究。

I combine physics-based hazard simulation and probabilistic risk modeling with large-scale mobility data and geospatial analysis. My current work includes generating synthetic commuting flows, modeling tropical cyclone wind–rain hazards under climate scenarios, and developing StormTailor to investigate compound-event probabilities. Alongside these studies, I explore the theoretical foundations of human–environment–disaster relationships. I am particularly interested in using urban analytics and GeoAI to connect climate extremes, infrastructure performance, and human responses to inform climate adaptation and disaster risk reduction.我结合基于物理过程的灾害模拟、概率风险建模、大规模移动数据与地理空间分析，开展合成通勤流生成、气候情景下的热带气旋风雨灾害模拟，并通过 StormTailor 探索复合事件的概率估计。同时，我也研究“人—地—灾”关系的理论基础。我的研究兴趣还包括城市分析与地理空间人工智能（GeoAI），希望将气候极端、基础设施功能和人群响应联系起来，为气候适应与灾害风险降低提供依据。

Research Projects科研项目

Global Road Assets and Commuting under Compound Tropical Cyclone Hazards热带气旋风雨复合灾害下的全球道路资产与通勤网络

I investigate how compound tropical cyclone hazards reshape physical road damage and commuting disruption across the world’s urban systems. I generate synthetic commuting origin–destination flows with GlODGen and cyclone tracks from CMIP6 climate environments, then link C15–TCR wind–rain simulations with road asset valuation and network assignment. The study examines where asset losses, disconnected journeys, and rerouting pressures differ, and how these patterns may shift in a warming climate.我研究热带气旋复合灾害如何影响全球城市道路的实体损失及通勤功能，利用 GlODGen 生成合成通勤 OD、基于 CMIP6 气候环境生成气旋路径，并将 C15–TCR 风雨模拟与道路资产估值、路网交通分配相连接，分析资产损失、出行不可达与绕行压力的空间差异及其在变暖气候下的变化。

StormTailor — Compound Tropical Cyclone Hazard ModelingStormTailor — 热带气旋复合灾害建模

I developed StormTailor to link a reproducible, multiyear synthetic cyclone generator with regional wind and rainfall models. The framework investigates risk stratification and importance sampling for estimating compound-event probabilities. It identifies wind and rainfall extremes at shared locations and time windows, carries sampling weights into model-conditional probability estimates, and compares physical simulations with historical storms and public observations in Hong Kong.我开发 StormTailor，将可重放的多年合成气旋生成器与区域风场、降雨模型连接，探索通过风险分层和重要性抽样估计复合事件概率。框架在相同位置与时间窗口内识别风雨极端事件，以抽样权重估计基础模式气候中的条件概率，并结合香港历史台风和公开观测检验物理模拟。

Tropical Cyclone Risk, Healthcare Supply, and Older Populations热带气旋风险、医疗服务供给与老年人口

I assess older-adult exposure, potential hospital asset losses, and healthcare supply mismatches under tropical cyclone winds across 52 Chinese coastal cities. The analysis combines cyclone wind fields with population and hospital data to examine how climate change and population aging jointly reshape risk. Under SSP5-8.5, annual hospital asset losses are projected to more than double, while climate change amplifies aging-driven exposure growth by 19.7–32.8%.我在中国52个沿海城市评估热带气旋风灾下的老年人口暴露、医院资产潜在损失与医疗服务供需错配，结合气旋风场、人口和医院数据，分析气候变化与人口老龄化对风险格局的共同影响。SSP5-8.5情景下，年度医院资产损失预计将超过翻倍；气候变化使老龄化驱动的暴露增长进一步增加19.7–32.8%。

15-Minute Service Accessibility during Urban Floods城市洪水情境下的15分钟服务可达性

I investigate how the spatial distribution of everyday services, flood-induced network disruption, and human mobility jointly shape neighborhood access. Comparing Shenzhen and Zhengzhou, I combine pedestrian-network accessibility models with mobile-phone origin–destination data to distinguish potential access losses from observed changes in arrivals at service-containing neighborhoods.我关注日常服务的空间配置、洪水造成的路网中断与人群移动如何共同塑造社区可达性。以深圳和郑州为案例，我结合步行网络可达性模型与手机信令 OD 数据，区分模型预测的潜在可达性损失与包含服务设施的社区实际到达量变化。

Early Warning, Shelter-in-Place, and Human Mobility灾害预警、就地避险与人群移动

How do warning timing and urban spatial organization shape protective responses? We compare hourly, 1-km mobile-phone origin–destination flows in Shenzhen during Typhoon Mangkhut and Zhengzhou during the July 2021 rainstorm. The study examines response delays, mobility suppression, and proximity to emergency shelters. I process the mobility data, construct agent-based models of information diffusion and behavioral response, and revise the manuscript, investigating how the population’s initial distribution between home and work affects shelter-in-place and movement patterns.预警时机与城市空间组织如何影响人群避险响应？我们利用深圳“山竹”台风和郑州“7·20”暴雨期间逐小时、1公里网格的手机信令 OD，比较响应时滞、活动抑制及其与应急避难场所邻近性的关系。我承担移动数据处理、信息传播与行为响应的主体模型构建以及论文修改，探究初始职住分布如何影响就地避险与移动模式。

Human–Environment–Disaster Relationships and Urban Resilience“人—地—灾”耦合关系与城市韧性

I explore the geographical foundations of urban resilience by treating disaster risk as an endogenous constraint on human–environment relationships. This theoretical work examines how human activities, environmental endowments, and disaster impacts interact, and uses the 2021 Zhengzhou flood to discuss how adaptation and risk constraints reshape urban systems.我从地理学视角探索城市韧性的理论基础，将灾害风险视为人地关系演化的内生约束，分析人类活动、环境禀赋与灾害影响之间的相互作用，并结合2021年郑州洪灾，讨论适应行为与风险约束如何共同塑造城市系统。

Urban Mobility, Emergency Resources, and Regional Resilience人群移动、应急资源与区域韧性

I reconstructed Typhoon Mangkhut’s wind and rainfall evolution with ER11 and GMCP and processed 2.5 billion mobile-phone records to examine coastal–inland differences in urban response. My collaborative work also addresses shelter demand–supply mismatches across 21 cities using GHS-SMOD, an interactive emergency resource allocation system, and dynamic Bayesian network–ARIMA models of resilience trajectories across nine Pearl River Delta cities.我利用 ER11 和 GMCP 重建台风“山竹”的风雨演变，处理25亿条手机信令记录，分析沿海与内陆地区城市响应的差异。合作研究还包括利用 GHS-SMOD 识别21个城市的避难场所供需错配、开发应急资源配置交互式系统，以及结合动态贝叶斯网络与 ARIMA 分析珠三角9个城市的韧性演变。
